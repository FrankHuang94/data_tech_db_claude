## Findings

- **Databricks valuation history**, verified in this sweep:

  | Date | Round | Valuation |
  |---|---|---|
  | Feb 2019 | Series E | $2.75B |
  | Oct 2019 | Series F | $6.2B |
  | Feb 2021 | Series G | $28B |
  | Aug 2021 | Series H | $38B |
  | Sep 2023 | Series I | $43B |
  | Dec 2024 | Series J ($10B equity, plus $5.25B debt in Jan 2025, when Meta joined) | $62B |
  | Aug–Sep 2025 | Series K ($1B) | >$100B |
  | Dec 2025 | Series L (>$4B announced) | $134B |
  | Feb 2026 | Series L completed (~$5B equity plus ~$2B debt capacity) | $134B |
  | Jul 2026 | Coatue-led term sheet | $188B |
  | Aug 13, 2026 | Coatue-led close ($5B) | $190B |

  The value roughly tripled from Series J to the August 2026 close in about 20 months. Over the same stretch the company-stated revenue run-rate went from $4B (announced Aug–Sep 2025) to $4.8B, then $5.4B, then more than $7B. That puts the implied multiple at about 27x run-rate at $190B. This is my own arithmetic on company-stated run-rate, not a reported figure. No employee tender offer was found for 2026 `[VERIFY]`.

- **Flat or stale since 2021–22, with no new priced round found:** Fivetran ($5.6B), dbt Labs ($4.2B), Starburst, Dremio ($2B), Cockroach Labs ($5B), Collibra ($5.25B), Monte Carlo ($1.6B; Tracxn estimates $1.32B for Jan 2026, which is not a priced round), Pinecone ($750M, 2023; reportedly explored a sale), Weaviate and Firebolt. These 2021-vintage marks probably sit above what the companies would fetch today. The Fivetran–dbt all-stock merger, announced Oct 2025, avoided a new price test.
- **Acquired or bought out since their last round:**

  | Company | Buyer | Price | Timing |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Neon | Databricks | ~$1B reported | May 2025 |
  | Tecton | Databricks | not given | 2025 |
  | Weights & Biases | CoreWeave | ~$1.7B | closed May 2025 |
  | SingleStore | Vector Capital (growth buyout) | not given | Sep 2025 |
  | dbt Labs | merged with Fivetran | all-stock, no price | merger said to have closed Jun 1, 2026 `[VERIFY]` |
  | Anyscale | Nscale | ~$1.65B | announced Jul 30, 2026 |
  | Dagster | Prefect | not given | agreed Jul 2026 `[VERIFY]` |

  Scale AI was not acquired outright: Meta bought a 49% non-voting stake.
- **Hottest categories in 2025–2026:**
  - AI-era operational and agent infrastructure: Supabase, Temporal, Databricks Lakebase and Neon.
  - Real-time analytics: ClickHouse.
  - Data security and DSPM: Cyera.
  - AI data infrastructure and storage: VAST Data, Hammerspace.
  - Document ETL for LLMs: Reducto.
  - AI training data: Scale AI and Meta; Surge AI was reported in talks at $15–25B in Jul 2025, but no close was confirmed, so it is not in the CSV.

  Vector-database pure plays raised small rounds (Qdrant $50M, LanceDB $30M), which looks like consolidation and commoditization of that category.
- **Secondary liquidity is a recurring feature of these rounds:** the Databricks Series J (employee RSU taxes), VAST Series F (about 50% secondary) and Cribl Series E ($119M secondary).
