- **Total addressable market** — the revenue available if a product won every possible customer in its defined category (TAM); company-stated TAMs are unaudited. (ch41)
- **Serviceable addressable market** — the part of a TAM a product can actually reach with its current offer, channels and geography (SAM). (ch41)
- **End-user spending** — a market-sizing basis that counts what buyers pay, including hardware, devices and services, rather than what software vendors book. (ch41)
- **Vendor revenue basis** — a market-sizing basis that sums the revenue vendors book in a category, as in Gartner's market-share reports. (ch41)
- **Syndicated research** — market reports sold to many buyers by research houses, often with opaque methods and wide disagreement between houses. (ch41)
- **Double counting** — adding the same spend more than once across layers of a value chain, such as a SaaS vendor's revenue and the cloud fees it pays. (ch41)
- **dbPaaS** — a database offered as a managed cloud service (database platform as a service), now the majority of DBMS spending. (ch41)
- **FinOps** — the practice of managing and reducing cloud spend through shared cost accountability between engineering and finance. (ch41)
- **Sovereign cloud** — cloud capacity operated under local jurisdiction and control to meet data-residency and national-security requirements. (ch41)
