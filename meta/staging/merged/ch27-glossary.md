- **Enterprise value** — Market capitalisation plus debt minus cash, the value of the operating business to all capital providers. (ch27)
- **EV to NTM revenue** — Enterprise value divided by expected revenue over the next twelve months, the standard software valuation multiple. (ch27)
- **Growth-adjusted multiple** — A valuation multiple divided by the revenue growth rate, used to compare companies growing at different speeds. (ch27)
- **Post-money valuation** — The latest private round's price per share multiplied by all fully diluted shares after the round. (ch27)
- **Illiquidity discount** — The reduction in value applied to shares that cannot be freely sold on a public market. (ch27)
