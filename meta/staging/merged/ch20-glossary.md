- **Reinforcement learning from human feedback** — tuning a model with a reward signal learned from human preference rankings of its outputs (RLHF). (ch20)
- **RL environment** — a sandboxed task with an automatic grader that a model practices on during reinforcement learning, sold by data vendors to labs. (ch20)
- **Expert network** — a data vendor that rents credentialed specialists (PhDs, doctors, engineers) by the hour to write or grade training data. (ch20)
- **Synthetic data** — training data generated by a model rather than collected from people or the world. (ch20)
- **Distillation** — training a smaller "student" model on the outputs of a larger "teacher" model. (ch20)
- **Model collapse** — progressive degradation when models train recursively on model-generated data and lose the rare tails of the real distribution. (ch20)
- **MinHash** — a compact document signature whose overlap with another signature estimates the two documents' Jaccard similarity. (ch20)
- **Jaccard similarity** — the size of two sets' intersection divided by the size of their union, used to measure near-duplicate text. (ch20)
- **Locality-sensitive hashing** — hashing that puts similar items into the same bucket so only likely matches are compared (LSH). (ch20)
- **Semantic deduplication** — removing documents with near-identical meaning, even when worded differently, by clustering their embeddings. (ch20)
- **Quality classifier** — a small model that scores documents for usefulness (e.g., educational value) so a corpus can be filtered. (ch20)
- **Data mixture** — the proportions of each data source (web, code, math, books, synthetic) used at each stage of model training. (ch20)
- **Benchmark decontamination** — removing evaluation questions and answers from training data so benchmark scores are not inflated. (ch20)
- **Fair use** — the US copyright defense that weighs a use's purpose, the work's nature, the amount used and the effect on the market. (ch20)
- **Acquihire** — a deal whose main purpose is hiring a team rather than buying a business or its products. (ch20)
- **Take rate** — the share of gross billings a marketplace or staffing-style vendor keeps after paying its contractors. (ch20)
