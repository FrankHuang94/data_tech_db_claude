- **NTM revenue multiple** — Enterprise value divided by next-twelve-months revenue (usually from guidance or consensus), the standard valuation yardstick for growth software. (ch37)
- **Rule of 40** — Revenue growth rate plus free-cash-flow (or operating) margin, with 40% or more read as a healthy balance of growth and profitability. (ch37)
- **OPM backsolve** — An option-pricing-model method that infers the value of each share class, including common, from the price of the latest preferred round. (ch37)
- **Fair value hierarchy** — The ASC 820 ranking of valuation inputs: Level 1 quoted prices, Level 2 observable inputs, Level 3 unobservable model inputs such as private-round marks. (ch37)
- **Control premium** — The amount an acquirer pays above a target's undisturbed share price to obtain control of the company. (ch37)
- **Deal spread** — The gap between a target's trading price and the agreed acquisition price, reflecting time value and the risk the deal fails. (ch37)
- **Multiple compression** — A fall in valuation multiples (such as EV/NTM revenue) that is driven by rates or sentiment rather than by changes in fundamentals. (ch37)
- **Enterprise value** — Equity value plus debt (and preferred/minority interests) minus cash, i.e., the value of the operating business. (ch37)
- **Growth-adjusted multiple** — A valuation multiple divided by the growth rate, giving the turns of revenue paid per point of growth. (ch37)
- **Post-money valuation** — A private company's round price per share times fully diluted shares outstanding after the round. (ch37)
- **Precedent transaction** — A completed or announced acquisition used as a benchmark for the price of a control deal. (ch37)
- **Take-private** — An acquisition, usually by a private-equity firm, that buys out public shareholders and delists the company. (ch37)
- **Secondary sale** — A sale of existing shares by current holders, so no new money reaches the company. (ch37)
- **Net revenue retention** — Revenue today from a cohort of customers one year ago, divided by that cohort's revenue a year ago. (ch37)
- **Revenue run-rate** — The latest month's or quarter's revenue annualized; not the same as recognized revenue. (ch37)
- **Liquidation preference** — The right of preferred shareholders to get their investment back (or a multiple of it) before common holders in a sale or liquidation. (ch37)
