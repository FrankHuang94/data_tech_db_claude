- **Gross revenue retention** — Share of a prior-year cohort's spend kept after contraction and churn, excluding expansion, capped at 100%. (ch36)
- **Net ARR expansion rate** — MongoDB's ARR-based retention metric: current ARR from year-ago customers divided by year-ago ARR from all customers. (ch36)
- **Cohort analysis** — Tracking a fixed group of customers over time to separate growth from the existing base from growth from new customers. (ch36)
- **Remaining performance obligations** — Contracted revenue not yet recognized, disclosed in the revenue footnote under ASC 606; often abbreviated RPO. (ch36)
- **Current remaining performance obligations** — The part of RPO a company expects to recognize as revenue within the next twelve months (cRPO). (ch36)
- **Capacity contract** — A prepaid or committed amount of consumption credits that a customer draws down as it uses a cloud service. (ch36)
- **Billings** — Revenue plus the change in deferred revenue; the amount invoiced in a period. (ch36)
- **Book-to-bill** — Orders received divided by revenue shipped in a period; above 1 means backlog is growing. (ch36)
- **Product gross margin** — Gross margin on platform (product) revenue only, excluding professional services. (ch36)
- **Cloud COGS** — Hyperscaler compute, storage and network costs that a cloud software vendor books in its cost of revenue. (ch36)
- **Magic number** — Annualized new revenue in a quarter divided by the prior quarter's sales and marketing spend; a test of go-to-market efficiency. (ch36)
- **CAC payback** — Months of gross profit from new revenue needed to recover the sales and marketing spent to acquire it. (ch36)
- **Stock-based compensation** — Employee pay in shares or options; expensed under GAAP, added back in non-GAAP results, and paid for by shareholders through dilution. (ch36)
- **Non-GAAP operating margin** — Operating margin after adding back stock-based compensation, amortization of acquired intangibles and other company-defined items. (ch36)
- **Free cash flow margin** — Operating cash flow minus capital expenditure, divided by revenue. (ch36)
- **Adjusted free cash flow** — Free cash flow with company-specific add-backs, such as payroll taxes paid on employee equity transactions. (ch36)
- **Rule of 40** — Heuristic that a software company's revenue growth rate plus profit margin (usually FCF margin) should be at least 40%. (ch36)
- **Annual recurring revenue** — The annualized value of recurring subscription or committed contracts at a point in time (ARR). (ch36)
