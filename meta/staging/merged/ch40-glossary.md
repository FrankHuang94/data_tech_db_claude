- **Cloud marketplace** — A hyperscaler's storefront (AWS Marketplace, Microsoft Marketplace, Google Cloud Marketplace) where third-party software is bought and billed on the cloud bill. (ch40)
- **Private offer** — Custom price and terms that a seller publishes to one buyer's cloud-marketplace account. (ch40)
- **Channel partner private offer** — An AWS Marketplace private offer (CPPO) that a reseller or SI transacts on the software vendor's behalf, at an extra 0.5-point fee. (ch40)
- **Listing fee** — The percentage of contract value a cloud marketplace keeps on each transaction, now 1.5–3% for most private offers. (ch40)
- **Committed-spend drawdown** — Consuming a prepaid or promised spend commitment through usage or eligible purchases, including marketplace software. (ch40)
- **Enterprise Discount Program** — AWS's multi-year committed-spend contract (EDP, also Private Pricing Agreement) that trades a spend commitment for discounts. (ch40)
- **Azure Consumption Commitment** — Microsoft's committed-spend agreement (MACC) toward which "Azure benefit eligible" marketplace purchases count 100%. (ch40)
- **Committed use discount** — Google Cloud's discount (CUD) for committing to a level of resource use or spend over a term. (ch40)
- **Co-sell** — Joint selling in which a hyperscaler's or partner's sellers help close a vendor's deal and earn credit for it. (ch40)
- **Quota retirement** — Counting a partner's marketplace deal toward a hyperscaler seller's own sales quota, the main co-sell incentive. (ch40)
- **ISV Accelerate** — AWS's co-sell programme for software vendors that list and sell through AWS Marketplace. (ch40)
- **Market development funds** — Vendor money (MDF) given to partners for joint marketing and demand generation. (ch40)
- **Deal registration** — A partner's formal claim on a sales opportunity that protects its credit, margin and incentives. (ch40)
- **Systems integrator** — A consulting firm (SI) that designs and implements technology projects and often steers which platform a client picks. (ch40)
- **Partner-sourced revenue** — Revenue from deals a partner originated, as distinct from partner-influenced deals it only assisted. (ch40)
- **Reference architecture** — A published, tested design that combines specific products for a stated workload, with sizing guidance. (ch40)
- **OEM partnership** — An arrangement in which one company embeds or bundles another's product and sells it under its own brand. (ch40)
- **S3-compatible storage** — Non-AWS object storage that implements the Amazon S3 API closely enough for engines and tools to use it. (ch40)
- **Data marketplace** — A data platform's storefront where providers list datasets, models and apps for that platform's customers. (ch40)
- **NVIDIA AI Data Platform** — NVIDIA's 2025 reference design that storage partners build into AI-ready storage products. (ch40)
- **NVIDIA-Certified Storage** — NVIDIA's storage validation programme with Foundation, Enterprise and NCP levels. (ch40)
