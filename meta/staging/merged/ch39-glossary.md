- **Acquihire** — an acquisition made mainly to hire the target's team, often shutting down its product. (ch39)
- **License-and-hire** — a deal that licenses a startup's technology and hires its leaders without buying control of the company (e.g., Google–Windsurf, 2025). (ch39)
- **Take-private** — a buyout, usually by private equity, that delists a public company. (ch39)
- **Re-IPO** — a return to public markets by a company that was previously taken private (e.g., Informatica, 2021). (ch39)
- **Tuck-in acquisition** — a small acquisition folded into an existing product line, usually with an undisclosed price. (ch39)
- **Merger of equals** — an all-stock combination of similar-sized companies with no large control premium. (ch39)
- **Holdback** — part of deal consideration withheld or tied to continued employment, often expensed as compensation rather than counted in the price. (ch39)
- **Equity value** — the price paid for all of a company's shares; enterprise value adds net debt. (ch39)
- **Enterprise value** — equity value plus debt minus cash; the value of the operating business. (ch39)
- **Goodwill impairment** — a write-down of acquisition goodwill when the acquired business is worth less than its carrying value. (ch39)
- **Standard-essential patent** — a patent that must be licensed to implement an industry standard such as a cellular protocol. (ch39)
- **Modern data stack** — the 2018–2022 cloud toolchain of separate ingestion, warehouse, transformation, BI and activation vendors. (ch39)
