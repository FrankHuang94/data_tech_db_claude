- **GAAP** — US Generally Accepted Accounting Principles; in this atlas, the label for figures reported in SEC filings or earnings releases under those rules. (ch00-1)
- **Fiscal year** — the 12-month period a company reports on, which often does not match the calendar year (Snowflake's FY2027 = Feb 2026–Jan 2027). (ch00-1)
- **Depth label** — the atlas's L1 (vocabulary), L2 (mechanism) or L3 (implementation) marker for how far down an explanation goes. (ch00-1)
- **Press-reported** — a figure reported by business press but not confirmed by the company; one of the atlas's five number labels. (ch00-1)
- **Company guidance** — management's own forecast of a future period's revenue or margin, issued with earnings results. (ch00-1)
- **Market map** — a diagram that places companies and projects in the layers of an industry stack. (ch00-2)
- **Control point** — a layer that every other component must pass through, which gives its owner pricing and policy power (in data: the catalog, the semantic layer, the agent interface). (ch00-2)
- **Revenue run-rate** — the latest month's or quarter's revenue annualised; for private companies, company-stated and unaudited. (ch00-1)
- **Annual recurring revenue** — contracted subscription revenue expressed as an annual amount. (ch00-1)
- **Product revenue** — Snowflake's consumption revenue line, excluding professional services. (ch00-1)
- **Non-GAAP operating margin** — operating margin after company adjustments, usually excluding stock-based compensation and amortisation. (ch00-1)
- **Post-money valuation** — a private company's implied equity value immediately after a funding round. (ch00-1)
- **Enterprise value** — equity value plus debt minus cash; the numerator of most public valuation multiples. (ch00-1)
- **EV to NTM revenue** — enterprise value divided by next-twelve-months revenue. (ch00-1)
- **Lakehouse** — open tables on object storage, queried by many engines. (ch00-1, ch00-2)
- **Technical catalog** — the service that maps table names to current metadata and enforces access. (ch00-1, ch00-2)
- **Open table format** — a metadata layer that turns files on object storage into a transactional table. (ch00-2)
- **Business catalog** — a search, glossary and stewardship tool that helps people find and trust data. (ch00-2)
- **Semantic layer** — governed definitions of metrics and dimensions that compile into correct SQL. (ch00-2)
- **Vector database** — a database specialised in storing embeddings and finding nearest neighbours. (ch00-2)
- **Feature absorption** — when a standalone product category becomes a feature of a larger platform. (ch00-2)
- **Model Context Protocol** — the open standard for connecting AI agents to tools and data sources. (ch00-2)
- **Value migration** — the shift of profit from one layer of an industry to another. (ch00-2)
- **Data gravity** — the pull that large datasets exert on compute and applications because moving them is costly. (ch00-2)
- **Hyperscaler** — one of the largest public-cloud providers (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle). (ch00-2)
